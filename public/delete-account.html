Privacy & Data

Delete Your MilesSpot Account

If you want to permanently delete your MilesSpot account and associated data, email our support team using the address below. We will verify your request and process it in accordance with applicable law and our retention obligations.

Request account deletion: spotlight.support44@gmail.com

How to request deletion

Send an email with the subject Delete My MilesSpot Account. Include your MilesSpot username and the email address associated with your account so we can locate and verify it. Do not include your password.

Processing time: We aim to process verified requests within 30 days. We may contact you if we need additional information to verify your request.

Data that may be deleted

Subject to verification and applicable legal or security requirements, deletion may cover:

  • Account identifiers and profile details
  • Posts and uploaded media associated with your account
  • Stories and notes associated with your account
  • Messages and social relationship data associated with your account, where deletion is technically and legally applicable
  • Bookmarks and saved preferences

Some copies of messages or content already received by other users may remain visible to those users, and limited records may be retained where required for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution.
