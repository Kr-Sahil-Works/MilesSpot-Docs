Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how MilesSpot collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our mobile application and related services.

1. About this policy

MilesSpot (“MilesSpot,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides social, messaging, and multiplayer features. This policy applies to information processed through the MilesSpot app and services covered by this policy. The information processed depends on the features you use and the permissions you grant.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use MilesSpot, we may process these categories of information:

  • Account information: identifiers and account details provided by you or your sign-in provider, such as user ID, name, email address, and profile information.
  • Profile and social content: profile details, posts, images, messages, reactions, and content you choose to submit or share.
  • Communications and activity: messages, delivery/read states, chat participation, game-room activity, reports, and interactions needed to operate the service.
  • Device and technical information: app version, device or operating-system details, diagnostic information, and technical logs where available and necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the app.
  • Permission-based information: photos or images you select or provide when using features that require access to your device’s media library. Manage permissions in your device settings.
  • Support and safety information: information you send when contacting support or reporting content, accounts, or behavior.
Please avoid sharing passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, or other sensitive information in public profiles, posts, or messages.

3. How we use information

As applicable, we use information to:

  • Create and manage accounts and authenticate users.
  • Provide chats, social features, posts, profiles, and multiplayer experiences.
  • Deliver notifications and maintain service functionality.
  • Store preferences and support features you choose to use.
  • Respond to support requests and user reports.
  • Detect abuse, enforce safety rules, protect users, and secure the service.
  • Diagnose errors, maintain performance, and improve reliability.
  • Meet legal obligations and respond to valid legal requests.

4. When information may be shared

We do not sell personal information for money. We may share limited information as necessary:

  • With other users: information and content you choose to make visible through your profile, posts, chats, or social features.
  • With service providers: providers that help operate authentication, hosting, databases, notifications, or other app infrastructure, subject to applicable safeguards.
  • For safety and legal reasons: to investigate abuse, protect users or rights, comply with law, or respond to valid legal process.
  • Business changes: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or transfer of services, subject to appropriate protections and applicable law.

Information shared with other users may be copied or retained by recipients. Consider carefully what you post or send.

5. Data retention and account deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Retention periods depend on the type of information and why it is processed.

You can request deletion of your MilesSpot account and associated data using the account deletion option in the app, where available, or through this page:

Account deletion request:
https://milesspot-docs.onrender.com/delete-account

After verifying a request, we will delete or de-identify information associated with the account, except information we must retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or other lawful purposes. Any retained information will be handled under applicable law.

Uninstalling the app does not by itself submit an account deletion request.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure, and we cannot promise absolute security. Contact us if you believe your account has been accessed without authorization.

7. Children and safety

MilesSpot is not intended for children below the minimum age permitted by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited. If you believe a child has provided personal information in violation of applicable requirements, contact us so we can review the report.

We prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation. Users can report inappropriate content or behavior through available in-app reporting tools or by contacting us.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

  • Review and update information through available account settings.
  • Manage photo/media and notification permissions in your device settings.
  • Request account deletion using the link above.
  • Contact us with privacy questions or requests.

We may need to verify your identity before responding to a privacy request.

9. Third-party services and links

MilesSpot may rely on third-party services for parts of the app, such as authentication, hosting, or notifications. Those providers may process information under their own terms and privacy notices. External websites linked from MilesSpot are governed by their own policies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services or legal requirements change. We will update the “Last updated” date when changes are made. Where required, we will provide additional notice or seek consent.
